About Us

The MV Restaurant began in 2012 in a small kitchen, where our founder cooked her grandmother's recipes for neighbours every Sunday. The crowd grew week after week, so we opened a restaurant of our own. Today we still make our spice blends in-house and cook our curries slowly, the way they were cooked at home. Come hungry, and bring the whole family.
